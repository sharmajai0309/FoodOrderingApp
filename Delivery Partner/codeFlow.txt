You are a senior backend architect designing a Delivery Management System for a food ordering platform.

The system must integrate with an existing Restaurant Service and follow an event-driven architecture.

Your task is to design the entire delivery system before implementation. The output must be structured and production-ready.

---

SYSTEM CONTEXT

The platform contains three independent services:

1. Restaurant Service
2. Delivery Service
3. Notification Service (future implementation)

The Restaurant Service manages orders and payments.

When an order is confirmed or paid, the Restaurant Service publishes an ORDER_CONFIRMED event.

The Delivery Service listens to this event and becomes responsible for assigning a delivery partner and managing the delivery lifecycle.

---

DATABASE ARCHITECTURE

The Delivery Service uses two databases:

PostgreSQL is used for transactional business data such as:

• delivery partner profiles
• delivery assignments
• delivery partner documents
• driver earnings
• partner availability

MongoDB is used for event-driven data such as:

• delivery event logs
• delivery activity timeline
• delivery status history
• system activity logs

This polyglot persistence architecture improves performance, scalability, and separation of workloads.
